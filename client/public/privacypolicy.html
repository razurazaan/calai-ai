CalSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

CalSnap helps you track the food you eat and how much you move. It is made by Fantek, India. This page explains what data CalSnap collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it. It applies to the CalSnap website and the Android and iPhone apps.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and password. The password is stored only in hashed form, so nobody can read it.
  • Profile: gender, age, height, weight, target weight, activity level and goal, used to calculate your daily calorie and macro targets.
  • Food log: the meals you log, with calories and nutrients, and a small thumbnail of any food photo you scan.
  • Weight and steps: the weight entries you add and your daily step totals.
  • Usage: when you last used CalSnap, from which platform (website, Android or iPhone), and how many AI scans you used, to apply daily scan limits and keep the service running.

Food photos and AI analysis

When you scan a meal, the photo (or the text you type) is sent to Google Gemini to estimate the food items, portions and nutrients. Google processes it to return the result, under the Gemini API terms. Only the photo or text is sent, never your name, email or profile. CalSnap keeps only a small thumbnail of the photo with the meal you save.

Camera and photos

CalSnap uses your camera or photo library only when you choose to scan a meal. It does not access them at any other time.

Health data (steps)

If you connect Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone), CalSnap reads only your daily step count. It does not read any other health data and never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health.

Your daily step totals are saved to your CalSnap account so you can see your step history on any device. Health data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

You can stop syncing at any time from the Steps screen in CalSnap, and remove CalSnap's access in Health Connect or Apple Health settings.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and do not show ads. Data is processed only by the services that run CalSnap: Google Gemini (meal analysis, as above), our cloud database (MongoDB Atlas) and our hosting provider (Render). All data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account at any time in the app or on the website: Settings → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, profile, food logs and photo thumbnails, weight entries, step history and AI scan history. See how to delete your CalSnap account.

The only thing kept is the record of any payment you made for CalSnap Pro (amount, date and payment reference), which the law requires us to keep for accounting. It is no longer linked to your name or email.

How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as you have an account, so you can see your history. When you delete your account, it is deleted as described above.

Children

CalSnap is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
