Delete your CalSnap account

CalSnap is made by Fantek. You can delete your CalSnap account and all of its data yourself, at any time, from the app or from this website. You don't need to install the app to do it.

On this website

  1. Open CalSnap Settings and log in with your email and password.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete permanently.

Go to Settings

In the Android or iPhone app

Open CalSnap, go to Settings → Delete account, enter your password and confirm.

Can't log in?

Email snapcheckfehmeyal@gmail.com from the email address of your account and ask us to delete it. We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Deleting is immediate and permanent. It removes your account (name, email, password), profile, food logs and photo thumbnails, weight entries, step history and AI scan history.

The only thing kept is the record of any CalSnap Pro payment (amount, date and payment reference), which the law requires us to keep for accounting. It is no longer linked to your name or email.
